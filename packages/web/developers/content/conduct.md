# Rules for bots

A bot sees and can pass on what is in the channels it can read, so people have to be able to rely on it. These hold along with the [terms of use](/terms) of the instance you run it on.

## You answer for it

Your bot acts under your account's responsibility. If it does something the terms forbid, that is on you, and it stops when your account is suspended.

## What you owe people

- **Keep nothing you do not need,** and say what you keep. Messages are other people's words.
- **Pass nothing on** beyond what your bot is for, and sell nothing.
- **Say what it does** in its description, so people can judge before they add it.
- **Ask for the rights you need,** and no more.
- **Let people out.** If it stores anything about someone, they must be able to have it deleted.

Where data protection law applies to you, it applies to what your bot stores, and that is your responsibility and not the instance's.

## What is not allowed

- **Driving a person's account by a program** (a "self-bot"). Programs belong on a bot's account, which is marked as one.
- **Sending people things they did not ask for,** or pretending to be a person, the instance or another bot.
- **Getting around a limit, a ban or a space's rules,** for example with several bots doing what one may not.
- **Collecting** who is where, or what people write, beyond what the bot is for.

## What happens otherwise

A space can kick or ban a bot like anyone. An instance's operator can delete it, and can turn bots off altogether. If yours misbehaves, whoever notices can [report it](/report).
